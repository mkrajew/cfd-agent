# Journal: LLM capability spike (2026-10-29)

## 2026-10-29 experiment: capability spike of gpt-oss-120b and Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct

`scripts/llm_spike.py` ran four checks (Entra authentication, tool calling, strict JSON-schema output, `reasoning_effort` low and high) in 3 repeats per model on the OpenAI v1 route of the AI Services account in italynorth. Both models passed every check in 3 of 3 repeats, and Entra ID worked with the owner's CLI login on the first attempt. For `gpt-oss-120b` the mean completion tokens were 305 at low effort and 589 at high; Llama accepted the parameter but ignored it (268 and 260). The service reports no separate reasoning tokens. Mean latency was 0.4 to 2.0 s per call, and the whole spike cost under one cent. Raw data: `docs/adr/data/0002-llm-spike.json`.

## 2026-10-29 obstacle: qwen3-32b cannot be deployed

ARM rejected the `qwen3-32b` deployment for both `GlobalStandard` and `DeveloperTier` on the account, so the spike could not run for it. The comparison-model fallback rule offered a replacement (`Llama-4-Scout`, then `mistral-small`, then `Phi-4`); the decision below declines it.

## 2026-10-29 decision: M3 uses two models only

The owner decided not to deploy a replacement for `qwen3-32b`: `gpt-oss-120b` (primary) and `Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct` (comparison) are enough. The model-comparison row of the M3 matrix now covers Llama only, and the matrix estimate dropped from about 75 to 85 runs to about 65 to 75 (9 runs fewer); `reasoning_effort` is varied for `gpt-oss-120b` only. See ADR-0002 and design section 14.1.
